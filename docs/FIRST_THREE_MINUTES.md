# Alpha 0.87 — opening weapon follow-up

The first earned XP upgrade at or after 01:00 active play includes one eligible
card for the first weapon chosen: its next rank, or its evolution when rank 3 is
ready. The other two cards are random. This is one opportunity per run, not an
extra level or a forced selection. If the weapon is already evolved, the offer
stays random. Bonus rewards do not trigger it. A reroll or banish refresh replaces the whole offer
with ordinary random choices. The opening four-weapon choice, later offers, XP,
enemy stats, schedules, saves and training are otherwise unchanged.

The change addresses a progression risk observed in the Alpha 0.48 scripted
three-minute probes: primary rank 3 fell from 12/12 to 3/12 runs after guaranteed
repeats were removed. Those scripted runs are not human balance evidence.

Automated VM checks exercise offer timing, four paths, ready evolution, bonus
rewards, rerolls and retry. They cannot establish natural-play balance or phone
touch behavior; Robert's device observations decide acceptance.
